# Utility Commercial Analysis

By **Sustainable Energy Solution BD**. Developed by Istiak Ahmed Bappi.

A browser app for monthly electricity billing analysis (DPDC A7 bill data):

- Upload monthly `A7_Bill_Data_YYYYMM.xlsx` files; customer, payment and arrear columns are matched automatically.
- Arrear analysis of any bill month (payments of a bill come from the next month's file).
- Summaries by meter reader, supervisor, feeder and bill group, each with a full customer list and Excel download.
- Payment register and payment history across all months.
- Daily disconnection plan for field teams, WhatsApp lists and reminders, field results and disconnected list.
- Custom tabs, billed-unit filters, project file save/open.

## Privacy

All billing files are read and processed **inside your browser**. Nothing is uploaded to any server. Data is kept in the browser's storage on your computer; use **Save project** to keep a backup file.

## Use

Open the site, go to page 1 (Data) and upload your monthly bill files and meter reader list.
